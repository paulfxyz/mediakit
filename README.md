## Quick start

- **Writing about me?** Start with the [copy-ready biographies](docs/BIOGRAPHIES.md) and [life and work](docs/LIFE-AND-WORK.md).
- **Need a picture?** Use the [approved portrait](assets/portrait/paul-fleury-square-512.jpg), not an image found elsewhere. Read the [portrait guide](docs/PORTRAIT.md) and [media permission](LICENSE) first.
- **Covering a project?** Consult the [project index](docs/PROJECTS.md); each product has its own website and licensing.
- **Looking for personal context?** Read [a note on privacy and public presence](docs/PERSONAL-STATEMENT.md), in my own voice.
- **Need a different use?** Use an [official contact route](#official-contact-and-profiles) to request written permission for a use outside the [media permission](LICENSE).

## Background

My work spans connectivity, cybersecurity, AI, and independent products ([professional profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paulfxyz/)). The wider portfolio combines ventures, open-source tools, and smaller experiments ([personal website](https://paulfleury.com)).

Over the past 15 years, I have lived in at least a dozen countries, with a minimum of six months in each of the places listed in my [life-and-work account](docs/LIFE-AND-WORK.md). I genuinely enjoyed living in all of them; each city, region, and country was special and enriching in its own way. The list is non-chronological, without individual dates or more precise lengths of stay.

The [biographies](docs/BIOGRAPHIES.md) offer a one-line introduction, short professional and personal-background versions, and a fuller account. Choose the level of detail that fits the story.

For the context behind how I manage my public presence, read [A note on privacy and public presence](docs/PERSONAL-STATEMENT.md), in my own words. The balance I choose today can change over time.

**This is the only photograph of myself I authorise for public reuse.** Every portrait file in this repository is a size, format, or crop of that same approved image, not another photograph.

Other avatars or images may turn up online, but this kit authorises only the supplied portrait. It includes no video, alternative photograph, or high-resolution original.

| Version | Dimensions | Formats | Intended use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Square | 128 × 128 | PNG, JPEG, WebP | Small author bylines |
| Square | 256 × 256 | PNG, JPEG, WebP | Profiles and article cards |
| Square | 512 × 512 | PNG, JPEG, WebP | Largest authorised portrait export |
| Circle | 256 × 256; 512 × 512 | PNG, WebP with transparency | Round profile layouts |
| Editorial crop | 400 × 500 | PNG, JPEG, WebP | Compact 4:5 article portrait |

Browse [all portrait files](assets/portrait/) or use the [format-by-format download table](docs/PORTRAIT.md). The portrait is intentionally capped at 512 pixels on its longest edge; use the supplied size without upscaling, facial enhancement, or reconstruction.

## Using these materials

The [media permission](LICENSE) allows attributed editorial and biographical use of the supplied material under its stated conditions. Read it before reuse, especially for an event, altered image, advertisement, or endorsement.

- **Portrait, words, graphics, and identity:** Rights are reserved except for the limited permission in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- **Build code:** `scripts/` and the validation workflow are MIT-licensed under [LICENSES/MIT.txt](LICENSES/MIT.txt). That does not license the person or the generated assets.
- **Other projects:** Each linked project's own license and terms apply.
- **Copies may persist:** GitHub permits viewing and forking public repositories through its service; those copies may persist after the original changes ([GitHub terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service)).

This is not a public-domain dedication or a Creative Commons release of my likeness. Lawful exceptions, independent reporting, criticism, and GitHub's own platform rights remain unaffected.
