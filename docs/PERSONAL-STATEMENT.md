# A note on privacy and public presence

This is a personal statement by Paul Fleury, dated 12 October 2026. It shares only the context I choose to make public.

## Why I keep a low profile

I live with agoraphobia and claustrophobia, among other things I prefer to keep private. I am also a bit of a control freak and very privacy-conscious.

That is part of why I keep a low profile and mostly share projects and experiments. My life has not always been steady, and I sometimes react more strongly than I intend; clear boundaries help me maintain the control and balance I need to be effective and useful, personally and professionally.

My stance on a more open public presence is not fixed for life. I understand that the world is changing and that online presence increasingly matters in how people establish social trust.
